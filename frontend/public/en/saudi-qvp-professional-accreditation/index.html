Terms

Professional Accreditation, QVP, SVP and Mosadaqa

These names are close, but they are different services.

Saudi Professional Accreditation (الاعتماد المهني السعودي)The overall programme; it includes Professional Verification and the Skill Verification Program.
Professional Verification — Qualification Verification Program (QVP) (التحقق المهني (QVP))For professions that require a university degree: review of degrees, qualifications and experience.
Skill Verification Program (SVP) (الفحص المهني (SVP))For skilled trades: a test based on the job title in the visa.
Mosadaqa — educational certificate verification (مصادقة الشهادات (منصة «مصادقة»))A separate platform for verifying the educational certificates of non-Saudis; required for some professions.

Some people search for “professional attestation” or “المصادقة المهنية” for either Professional Verification or Mosadaqa. They are different services, and a profession may need one, both or neither.

Professional Verification

QVP for degree professions

QVP is a procedure within Saudi Professional Accreditation that reviews degrees, qualifications and experience for professions that require a university degree, such as engineers and specialists. It appears in the profession’s document list as “Professional Verification (QVP) certificate”.

SVP for skilled trades

The Skill Verification Program (SVP) is a test for skilled trades based on the job title in the visa. Our professional examination page (Arabic) explains the booking.

Mosadaqa for educational certificates

Mosadaqa is a separate Saudi platform for verifying the educational certificates of non-Saudis. It appears in the document list of some professions, such as engineers.

Questions

Frequently asked questions about QVP

What is the difference between Saudi Professional Accreditation and QVP?

Professional Accreditation is the Saudi programme that checks that a foreign worker has the qualifications and skills the profession requires. It has two services: Professional Verification (QVP) for professions that require a university degree, and the Skill Verification Program (SVP) for skilled trades. QVP is one part of Professional Accreditation, not all of it.

What is the difference between QVP and SVP?

QVP (Professional Verification) reviews degrees, qualifications and experience for degree professions. SVP is a test for skilled trades based on the job title in the visa. The job title in the visa decides which one applies.

Is Mosadaqa the same as Professional Verification?

No. Mosadaqa (mosadaqa.sa) is a separate Saudi platform for verifying the educational certificates of non-Saudis. It appears in the document list of some professions, such as engineers. QVP is a procedure within the Professional Accreditation programme. A profession may need one, both or neither.

How do I know whether my profession needs QVP, SVP or Mosadaqa?

Search your profession on the Professions and Documents page: each profession’s document list names the procedure it needs. Some professions, such as household workers, need neither QVP nor SVP. We review this with you before you apply.

Does the office issue the professional accreditation certificate?

No. The office is not the official platform or a government body. We review the profession and documents, explain the required procedure, and help prepare the documents, book the appointment and follow up. Registration, payment and the result go through the official platform and the competent authority.

Does the office guarantee passing the test or approval of the verification?

No. The test result and the verification decision belong to the competent official authority. Our role is to prepare and review the file before it is submitted.
