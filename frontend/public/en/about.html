Who we are

A Jordan-based team for Saudi visa and document coordination

Our office is located in Amman, Jordan, and works with people and organizations that need practical support with Saudi-related visa and document procedures.

Our main work is the processing and follow-up of Saudi work visa files for different professions. We help applicants and employers understand the documents connected with the intended job title, prepare a clearer file, arrange electronic authorization where applicable and follow the next step with the relevant office.

We also coordinate document attestation and legalization. This may include university certificates, school certificates, experience letters, civil documents, powers of attorney, contracts, commercial registers and medical reports. The required route depends on the issuing country, the type of document and the authority that will receive it.

International intermediaries—including recruitment firms, HR providers, travel businesses and visa service companies in India, Türkiye, the United Kingdom and other markets—can contact our Amman office when they need a local point of coordination for employees, applicants or document procedures in Jordan.

Our role: We provide processing support, document coordination and procedural guidance. Visa issuance, professional approval and final acceptance remain decisions of the competent authorities under the rules in effect for each case.

Professional platforms and verification

Guidance for profession-related Saudi platforms

Some professions may require a qualification verification process, a professional examination or registration with a Saudi professional body. We identify the applicable route for the intended profession instead of applying one rule to every applicant.

QVP / SVPI professional verificationReview whether the applicant needs a qualification or professional verification step for the intended job title.
Engineering registrationFor engineering cases, review the current requirements connected with the Saudi Council of Engineers or the relevant official service.
Educational certificate verificationWhen applicable, guide the applicant toward the official Mosadaqa certificate verification service and the documents it requests.
