B2B service scope

What we can coordinate

The exact workflow depends on nationality, residence status, visa purpose, intended profession, document origin and the competent authority's current requirements.

Employee work visa files

Profession-led case preparation

We help the employer and applicant understand which qualification, experience, age, attestation and professional verification requirements are connected with the intended job title.

Profession and applicant reviewNationality, Jordan residence status, intended profession, education, experience and age.
Document checklistA practical list for the applicant before originals are submitted or attestation work begins.
Authorization and follow-up guidanceCoordination of the electronic authorization and the next official or office step where applicable.
Document attestation

Jordan and cross-border document coordination

We review the expected route for university and school certificates, experience letters, birth and marriage records, divorce and death certificates, powers of attorney, commercial records, contracts, financial statements and medical reports issued in Jordan or abroad.

Jordanian authoritiesCoordination with the relevant issuing body, labour office, ministries and the Jordanian Ministry of Foreign Affairs where required.
Embassy and foreign-affairs stepsReview of the diplomatic and foreign-ministry sequence based on the issuing country and intended use, including Jordanian, Saudi, Emirati and other country routes where applicable.
Commercial and medical documentsCoordination guidance for company records, incorporation papers, financial statements, medical reports and other official documents.
Representative follow-upWhere the service is available, our representative can coordinate approved steps on behalf of the client or applicant.
Professional verification

Support for profession-dependent platforms

We help identify whether the case is connected with QVP, Musadaqa, the Saudi Council of Engineers or another current professional platform or competent body.

We do not apply the same platform to every profession. The named route is checked against the specific job title and current official requirements.
SVP Professional Verification ↗ Musadaqa ↗ Saudi Council of Engineers ↗
Other Saudi visa purposes

Visit, tourist, transit and Umrah pathway review

For non-work cases, share the applicant's nationality, residence country, travel purpose and number of applicants. We then identify the most relevant official pathway available at the time.

Eligibility and availability vary. A pathway review is not a promise of visa issuance or approval.
Transport and transit cases

Transit support for drivers and transport operators

We can review transit-related requests for truck drivers, road transport teams and other transport or air-transit cases based on nationality, route, vehicle or employer details and the current official pathway.

Transit eligibility depends on the route, nationality, documents and authority requirements. Contact us before making travel commitments.
Saudi company expansion

Jordanian company documents for Saudi expansion

For a company established in Amman and preparing to open or expand in Saudi Arabia, we can review the incorporation agreement, commercial register, current-year financial statement, residency or representative authorization and the required attestation sequence.

The financial-position page and current-year filing are important parts of the preliminary document review.

B2B questions

Can you support several employees or professions in one hiring project?

Yes. Share the intended professions, nationalities and approximate volume. We can then propose a checklist and communication workflow that keeps each applicant's requirements clear.

Can your representative coordinate attestation steps in Jordan?

Depending on the document and service availability, our representative can coordinate steps with relevant authorities. We confirm the exact scope before originals are delivered.

Do you guarantee a visa or official approval?

No. We coordinate processing, documents and follow-up. Visa issuance and official approvals remain the responsibility of the competent authorities under the rules in effect for the case.

Can you help with visit, tourist, transit or Umrah cases?

We can review the available pathway after receiving the applicant's nationality, residence country and travel purpose. Availability differs by case and current rules.
