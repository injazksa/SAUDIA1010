Saudi visa types

Which Saudi visa does your case need?

Work visas are our core service. For other purposes we review the official pathway with you; the requirements, fees and conditions are always those published by the Saudi authorities at the time of application.

Work visa (employment visa)

For an employee hired by a Saudi employer. The employer issues the visa through the Saudi Ministry of Human Resources platform (Qiwa) and authorizes our office; we prepare the occupation's documents and complete the procedure with the Saudi Embassy in Jordan.

Who decides: the Saudi Embassy and the competent authorities.

Executives and investors

Chairman, chief executive, general manager and investor titles follow their own record: a Saudi commercial register and investment licence instead of a degree and experience, an authorization through the Saudi investment authority, and age over 21 with no upper limit.

Who decides: the investment authority and the Saudi Embassy.

Domestic workers

Visas for domestic workers (such as household workers and private drivers) are issued through the Saudi Musaned platform. The documents in Jordan depend on the title; household titles need neither QVP nor SVP.

Who decides: Musaned and the Saudi Embassy.

Family visas

For residents of Saudi Arabia bringing their spouse and children. The family's marriage contract and birth certificates are attested — Jordanian Ministry of Foreign Affairs first, then the Saudi Embassy through our office — together with a police clearance and the Wafid medical examination (Family Visa) where required.

Who decides: the Saudi authorities under the family-visa rules in force.

Visit, tourist and business visit visas

Applications are made on the official Saudi visa platform of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (visa.mofa.gov.sa). Eligibility depends on nationality, residence and any visas held; we check the available pathway with you before you apply.

Who decides: the Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Umrah

Umrah visas and bookings are handled through Nusuk, the official platform of the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah. We help travellers follow the official requirements and prepare the documents they need.

Who decides: the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah through Nusuk.

Transit cases, including drivers and transport crews, depend on the route, nationality and current rules: share the details and we review the pathway before any travel commitment. Our English pages do not quote government fees or processing times, because they are set and changed by the authorities.

Work visa authorization and procedure

The Saudi work visa procedure in Jordan, step by step

After the Saudi employer issues the work visa, the file is completed in Jordan by an office that follows it with the Saudi Embassy. The electronic authorization (delegation) is the step that allows our office to do this. It does not guarantee the visa: the final decision belongs to the official authorities.

  1. 1

    Send the visa details

    Employee or employer: the visa document or its details, the occupation on the visa and the nationality, by WhatsApp or email. Office: receives them and starts reviewing the case.

  2. 2

    Case review

    Office: checks the information and sets the documents and steps for the occupation and the employee's situation.

  3. 3

    Electronic authorization to the office

    Employer or employee, depending on the case, completes the authorization; we explain how and provide the office details it needs. For some titles, such as chief executive, the authorization comes through the Saudi investment authority.

  4. 4

    Documents and attestation

    Employee: prepares the occupation's documents with their stamps. Office: reviews them and explains the order of stamps before submission.

  5. 5

    Medical examination and biometrics

    Employee: attends the medical examination and fingerprints at a Saudi-embassy-approved centre, with the original passport and two recent photos on a white background. Office: guides the procedure for the case.

  6. 6

    Hand in the file and passport

    Employee: delivers the completed documents and passport to the office. Office: checks the file is complete before submitting it.

  7. 7

    Follow-up with the Saudi Embassy

    Office: follows the transaction with the Saudi Embassy and informs you of any update.

  8. 8

    Passport collection

    Employee: collects the passport from the office once the procedure is finished.

Employment contract. Professional accreditation and verification

QVP, SVP, Mosadaqa and the professional bodies explained

Saudi Professional Accreditation is the Saudi program that checks a foreign worker's qualifications and skills against the occupation. It has two parts — Professional Verification (QVP) and the Skill Verification Program (SVP) — and some occupations also need a separate degree verification or a professional body.

Professional Verification (QVP)

Qualification Verification Program for occupations that require a comprehensive diploma or above (bachelor's, master's, doctorate). It reviews the certificates, qualifications and experience; the office service helps prepare and upload the certificates and experience letters, and requires at least two years of experience. Official platform: qvp.qiwa.sa.

Examples: engineers, accountants, marketing and business specialists, internal auditors, administrative titles.

Skill Verification Program (SVP)

For skilled and labour occupations that do not require a diploma or degree. It is a theory test only, with 25 to 30 multiple-choice questions (general and occupation-specific), and the result is pass or fail. In Jordan the test is taken at AMIDEAST; we book the appointment and follow up until the result. Official platform: svp-international.qiwa.sa.

Examples: construction, loading and packaging workers, cooks, warehouse workers, barbers, auto mechanics.

Mosadaqa degree verification

A separate Saudi platform for verifying the educational certificates of non-Saudis; it is not the same as QVP. In the office's records it is required for engineering occupations. Official platform: mosadaqa.sa.

Saudi Council of Engineers

The professional body that accredits and registers engineers. Engineering occupations need Mosadaqa, membership and a practising certificate from the Jordan Engineers Association, and Council registration. The Council's pages state that registration is not accepted with less than five years of experience unless the applicant is Saudi, Bidoon or the child of a Saudi mother, and that membership is for four-year engineering bachelor's degrees. Official site: saudieng.sa.

Health professions: SCFHS, Mumaris+ and DataFlow

Doctors, dentists, pharmacists, nurses and other health titles need professional classification and registration with the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS) through its Mumaris+ platform, after a DataFlow primary-source verification of certificates and experience. Some specialties also sit the Saudi licensing exam. In the office's records these replace QVP for health occupations.

Who needs none of these

Household and domestic occupations need neither QVP nor SVP, and executive and investor titles follow their own documents. The occupation on the visa decides: the occupation assistant below shows what applies to each title.

Registration, payment and results take place on the official platforms. Saudia Visa is not the platform or a government body and does not guarantee a test result or a verification decision.

Qualifications, experience and nationality

Employment visa requirements that depend on the employee

The occupation sets the minimum qualification, the experience and the age; the employee's nationality, residence and where the documents were issued decide some documents and stamps.

Qualification levels

  • University degree: bachelor's degree and transcript; professors also show a master's or doctorate if held.
  • Comprehensive diploma: reviewed by the office for degree occupations with QVP, with at least four years of experience.
  • Non-comprehensive or vocational diploma, secondary (Tawjihi) or Grade 10: meet the school-level requirements of skilled occupations.

Experience

  • Experience must be in the same title as the visa: an accountant's experience does not prove a marketing specialist's.
  • Most degree occupations ask for two years; some skilled titles ask for one. Only completed years count: one year and eleven months is not two years.
  • Experience letters are stamped by the Labour Office and the Jordanian Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Age

  • Between 21 and 60 for almost every occupation in the office's records.
  • Over 21 with no upper limit for executive and investor titles.

Certificates issued outside Jordan

  • A foreign certificate is not refused for that reason; only its stamps change: the issuing institution, the issuing country's foreign ministry and, where needed, its embassy in Jordan, then the Jordanian stamps.
  • Stamps must be recent and suitable for the year of the transaction.
  • Saudi citizens with a foreign engineering degree need an equivalency from the Saudi Ministry of Education instead of Mosadaqa, according to the Council of Engineers.

Nationality and residence

  • Non-Jordanians applying from Jordan need a valid Jordanian residence, or a document proving an accepted exception.
  • A Jordanian who also holds a foreign passport applies with the Jordanian requirements, whichever passport carries the visa.
  • Some documents differ by nationality; the occupation tools ask for it before showing the list.

Female applicants

  • Military service documents are replaced by a guardian's no-objection (or the marriage contract, depending on marital status).
  • The no-objection can come from a security centre, a court or abroad, and its stamps follow its source.

    Certificate attestation and document authentication

    Attesting certificates and documents for Saudi Arabia

    Documents used in a Saudi file must carry their stamps in the right order, on the original. The route depends on the document type and the country that issued it; we review it before you hand over any original.

    Issued in Jordan

    Issuing body → Jordanian MOFA → Saudi Embassy

    The document is stamped by the competent Jordanian body, then the Jordanian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and our office follows the Saudi Embassy attestation. For a university degree: the university, the Ministry of Higher Education, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, then the Saudi Embassy. For Jordanian birth, marriage, divorce and death certificates, a certified copy stamped by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs is enough.

    Issued in Saudi Arabia

    Saudi MOFA → Saudi consulate in Jordan → Jordanian stamps

    Saudi birth, marriage, divorce and death certificates need the original with the physical stamp of the Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs, then the Saudi consulate in Jordan through our office, then the Jordanian attestations for the document. A Saudi power of attorney attested electronically by the Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs is accepted as a copy.

    Issued in another country

    Issuing country's MOFA → Amman

    The original is stamped by the foreign ministry of the country that issued it (and, where required, its embassy in Jordan), then brought to Amman to complete the route for the document type.

    Documents we attest

    • Educational: bachelor's, master's and doctorate certificates, university transcripts, school certificates (Tawjihi, secondary), diplomas and training certificates.
    • Civil status: birth, marriage, divorce and death certificates.
    • Legal: general and special powers of attorney, dependency deeds, police clearance certificates, sale and purchase contracts.
    • Commercial and medical: commercial registers, incorporation and partnership agreements, financial statements, experience and employment certificates, and medical reports such as sick leave (without the names of medicines).

    Documents must be stamped by the Jordanian Ministry of Foreign Affairs before the Saudi Embassy attestation. Do not pay any fee before the office confirms what the document needs.

    Related document services

    • Full coordination: where the service is available, we handle the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of Justice, Higher Education, the Saudi Embassy and other embassies on your behalf.
    • Pick-up and delivery: we collect documents from your home or office, complete the attestation and return them to the same place.
    • Shipping: after attestation we can send documents by Aramex inside or outside Jordan, with tracking; the cost depends on destination and size and is confirmed before sending.
    • UAE Embassy attestation: we also prepare and follow documents that need the UAE Embassy in Amman, for travel, residence, work or company purposes.

    Questions from employers

    What does Saudia Visa do for employers?

    Saudia Visa Services Office – Jordan prepares and follows up Saudi work visa files for employees in Jordan: visa authorization, occupation and qualification review, document checklists and attestation, QVP and SVP guidance, Saudi Council of Engineers registration support, and medical and biometric coordination.

    Can you process work visas for many employees at once?

    Yes. We organise bulk employee processing as one company file with a dedicated coordinator. Companies with five or more employees, or larger recruitment rounds, can ask for a tailored corporate offer.

    What is Saudi work visa authorization (delegation)?

    After the Saudi employer issues the work visa, it authorizes an office electronically to complete the procedure in Jordan. Once the authorization reaches Saudia Visa, we check it against the employee's details and occupation and start the file.

    Which occupations need QVP and which need SVP?

    In the office's records, Professional Verification (QVP) appears for degree-based occupations and the Skills Verification Program test (SVP) for skilled trades. Health occupations need classification with the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties and a DataFlow report instead, and engineers also need Saudi Council of Engineers registration. Use the occupation assistant on this page to see what applies to each title.

    Is a comprehensive diploma treated like a bachelor's degree?

    Not automatically. For occupations that require a university degree and QVP, the office reviews a comprehensive diploma case by case, with at least four years of experience. A non-comprehensive or vocational diploma does not meet a university-degree requirement.

    Do you guarantee visa approval?

    No. Saudia Visa prepares, coordinates and follows up the file. Visa issuance, verification results and approvals are decided by the competent authorities under the rules in force.

    Do you recruit employees for companies?

    Our core activity is Saudi visa processing, authorization and documentation. For some companies with a clear job request, we may help nominate candidates on request, but Saudia Visa is not a recruitment agency.

    Is Saudia Visa accredited by the Saudi Embassy?

    Yes. Saudia Visa Services Office – Jordan is accredited by the Saudi Embassy in Jordan for work visa authorization and processing for all occupations, and also provides certificate attestation and professional verification services. It is an independent office, not a government body, and the final decision on every application belongs to the competent authority.

    Can employees who are not Jordanian apply through your office?

    Yes, if they apply from Jordan with a valid Jordanian residence, or with a document proving an accepted exception. Some documents and stamps change with nationality and with the country that issued the certificates.

    Do you help with family, visit or Umrah visas?

    Yes. For family visas we attest the family documents with the Saudi Embassy and guide the Wafid medical examination; visit and tourist visas are applied for on the official Saudi visa platform, and Umrah through Nusuk. We review the pathway that applies to the nationality and purpose before you apply.

    What do health professionals need?

    Doctors, dentists, pharmacists, nurses and other health titles need classification and registration with the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties through Mumaris+, after a DataFlow verification of their certificates and experience. These replace QVP for health occupations in the office's records.

    Which documents will each employee need?

    It depends on the occupation, nationality, residence and where the documents were issued. Every employee needs a police clearance, medical examination and biometrics, a meningitis vaccination certificate and photos; the qualification, experience, contract and verification documents vary by occupation. Masar Awraqi lists them for each case.
