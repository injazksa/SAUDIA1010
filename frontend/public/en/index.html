Documents issued in Jordan or abroad

Document attestation and legalization guidance

An overseas document is not ready for use simply because it has been translated or copied. The route depends on the issuing country, document type and receiving authority.

1

Identify the issuing authority

Confirm whether the document was issued by a university, school, employer, ministry, court or commercial authority.

2

Confirm the attestation sequence

We review the likely route through the issuing authority, the foreign ministry, the relevant diplomatic mission and the Jordanian authorities where required.

3

Coordinate the practical steps

Depending on the document and service availability, our representative can coordinate follow-up with ministries, foreign affairs departments, embassies or other competent authorities.

Frequently asked questions

Can you support an employer hiring several employees for Saudi Arabia?

Yes. We can discuss a structured case-coordination process for profession requirements, applicant documents, attestation and follow-up. Share the professions, nationalities and expected number of applicants for an initial review.

Can you attest documents without the employee visiting every authority?

Depending on the document, issuing country and service availability, our representative may coordinate follow-up with relevant authorities. We confirm what can be handled before originals are delivered.

Do you issue or guarantee the visa?

No. We provide processing and document coordination support. The competent authorities make the final decision under the rules in effect for the case.
